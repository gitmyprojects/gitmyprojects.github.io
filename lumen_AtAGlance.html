Why I built it
Business systems roles in manufacturing ask for SharePoint administration, Power Platform automation and experience with SOX and ISO controls. Those are hard to show with a single app, so I built a company to practice on: Lumen Photonics, with a headquarters and two plants, real reporting lines and the reference data an ERP would expect. Every control below was tested by signing in as the person it applies to, not as the administrator.

Controls Built In

  • Segregation of duties. Requesters can't approve, authors can't release their own documents, and the auditor can read everything but change nothing.
  • Fail-safe defaults. Every change request starts as SOX relevant, and only Business Systems can change that during triage.
  • Enforced, not just hidden. SharePoint has no column-level permissions, so a flow resets protected fields on every new request, and alerts Business Systems if it can't.
  • Controlled documents. Operators only ever see the approved revision, while drafts stay visible to their author and approver.
  • An audit trail. Versioning, check-in comments and run history record who changed what, and why.

Skills Demonstrated
